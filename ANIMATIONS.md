# Portfolio Animation Guide

Top 1 recommended animation from each category for maximum impact.

---

## 1. CSS TRANSFORM ANIMATIONS
**3D Perspective Tilt** — Cards rotate in 3D following mouse position, giving depth feel on hover.

---

## 2. CSS TRANSITION TYPES
**Cubic-bezier Overshoot** — Elements slightly overshoot their target then settle, feels springy and alive.

---

## 3. CSS KEYFRAME ANIMATIONS
**Bounce-in Reveal** — Elements scale from 0 with elastic bounce when entering viewport.

---

## 4. SCROLL-TRIGGERED ANIMATIONS
**Parallax Scrolling** — Background layers move at different speeds on scroll creating depth illusion.

---

## 5. CURSOR / MOUSE ANIMATIONS
**Magnetic Cursor** — Buttons and links warp toward your mouse as it approaches, like magnets.

---

## 6. TEXT ANIMATIONS
**Text Scramble on Scroll** — Characters randomly glitch then resolve into final text as you scroll into view.

---

## 7. IMAGE / MEDIA ANIMATIONS
**Image Reveal Wipe** — Images unclip from left-to-right (or any direction) as they enter viewport.

---

## 8. 3D ANIMATIONS
**3D Card Tilt** — Cards follow mouse with full 3D perspective, reflections, and depth layers.

---

## 9. SVG ANIMATIONS
**Path Drawing** — SVG icons and logos draw themselves with animated stroke-dashoffset on load.

---

## 10. LOADING / SKELETON ANIMATIONS
**Skeleton Shimmer** — Content placeholder with gradient shimmer sweep before real content loads.

---

## 11. SCROLLBAR ANIMATIONS
**Scroll Progress Bar** — Thin gradient line at top of page showing how far you have scrolled.

---

## 12. BACKGROUND ANIMATIONS
**Interactive Particle System** — Canvas particles that connect with lines when close and react to mouse.

---

## 13. HOVER ANIMATIONS
**Neon Glow** — Border and shadow pulse with colored glow on hover, cyberpunk feel.

---

## 14. CLICK / INTERACTION ANIMATIONS
**Ripple Effect** — Material Design ripple expands from click point across the element.

---

## 15. SECTION / PAGE TRANSITIONS
**Morph Transition** — Shape morphs between sections using clip-path animation on scroll.

---

## 16. FORM ANIMATIONS
**Floating Labels** — Input labels float up and shrink when field is focused or filled.

---

## 17. NAVIGATION ANIMATIONS
**Hamburger to X Morph** — Three lines smoothly animate into an X when mobile menu opens.

---

## 18. NOTIFICATION / TOAST ANIMATIONS
**Slide + Bounce In** — Notification slides from edge with slight bounce and auto-dismisses.

---

## 19. MODAL / OVERLAY ANIMATIONS
**Zoom + Blur** — Modal scales up from trigger point while background blurs and darkens.

---

## 20. DATA VISUALIZATION ANIMATIONS
**Counter Count-up** — Numbers animate from 0 to final value when scrolled into view.

---

## 21. PHYSICS-BASED ANIMATIONS
**Spring Animation** — Elements overshoot then bounce back with realistic spring physics (damping + stiffness).

---

## 22. GESTURE ANIMATIONS
**Swipe to Dismiss** — Cards and items can be swiped left/right to remove with physics-based momentum.

---

## 23. AUDIO-SYNCED ANIMATIONS
**Equalizer Bars** — Animated bars that pulse to music or ambient sound input.

---

## 24. WEBGL / THREE.JS ANIMATIONS
**Mouse-reactive Particles** — 3D particle cloud that shifts and flows following cursor movement.

---

## 25. CANVAS 2D ANIMATIONS
**Flow Field** — Thousands of particles following Perlin noise vectors creating organic flowing motion.

---

## 26. LIBRARY-SPECIFIC ANIMATIONS
**GSAP ScrollTrigger Timeline** — Complex multi-step animations triggered and scrubbed by scroll position.

---

## 27. MICRO-INTERACTIONS
**Like Button Heart Burst** — Heart icon scales up with particle explosion on click.

---

## 28. EASING FUNCTIONS
**Elastic Easing** — Overshoot + oscillation for bouncy, playful motion feel.

---

## 29. PERFORMANCE TECHNIQUES
**Intersection Observer Triggers** — Animations only fire when elements enter viewport, zero wasted frames.

---

## 30. ACCESSIBILITY ANIMATIONS
**prefers-reduced-motion** — All animations gracefully degrade for users who prefer reduced motion.

---
